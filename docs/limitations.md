# Limitations

The current release uses generated traffic and deterministic labels. Such evidence is useful for checking whether the assurance mechanism behaves as specified, but it cannot establish field effectiveness.

- Synthetic patterns may be easier to classify than operational traffic.
- Zeek processing is representative of the chosen telemetry path only.
- Offline labels are immediately available, unlike many deployed settings.
- Demonstration thresholds are engineering defaults, not accepted risk tolerances.
- No external partner has validated usability or operator decisions.
- No controlled cyber-range or operational trial has been completed.
